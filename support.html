Common questions

How do I back up my data?

CoinhakoCrypto stores everything locally. To back up, open Settings → Export Portfolio and choose one of the following:

  • Transactions CSV — every buy, sell, staking, airdrop, and transfer entry with fees and notes.
  • Holdings CSV — current positions and per-position P/L.
  • Summary Text — a plain-text snapshot suitable for messaging or archiving.

Save the exported file to Files, iCloud Drive, or share it to another app of your choice.

I lost my data after reinstalling — can you help?

Unfortunately, no. Because the App stores data locally and does not sync to any server, we cannot recover data that was removed with the previous install. Going forward, we recommend exporting a backup before uninstalling or before restoring your device.

The price shown doesn't match my exchange. Why?

Price information in CoinhakoCrypto is generated locally for demonstration and tracking context. It is not sourced from any exchange in real time. Please do not rely on the App for live trading, and always verify prices on the exchange or aggregator you trust before acting.

Can you add a feature that connects to my exchange?

CoinhakoCrypto is intentionally designed as a manual journaling tool and does not connect to any exchange, broker, or trading venue. We believe this is the right product boundary for an app whose job is to help you think, not to execute trades.

We do consider feature requests seriously. If you'd like to share one, please email us.

Is the App collecting any data about me?

No. CoinhakoCrypto does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. Please see our Privacy Policy for the full details.

How do I reset my preferences without losing transactions?

Open Settings → Reset Preferences. This only resets display and chart preferences — your transactions and holdings are not touched.

How do I delete all my data?

Use Settings → Reseed Sample Data to clear existing transactions and reload the demo set, or uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data.
