Frequently asked questions

Short, honest answers.

Does CoinhakoCrypto connect to any exchange?

No. The app is a manual journaling and analysis tool. It does not connect to any exchange, broker, or trading venue, and it cannot place orders.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device. There is no cloud sync and no server-side account. If you delete the app, the data is removed with it — please export first if you need a backup.

Is the price data real-time?

Price information shown in the app is generated locally for demonstration and tracking context. Do not rely on it for real-time price discovery or trade execution decisions.

Does the app ask for any permissions?

No. The app does not request notification, biometric, contacts, or location permissions. It runs entirely offline after the initial setup.

Can I export my data?

Yes. You can export your transactions and holdings to CSV, or generate a plain-text portfolio summary, from the Settings → Export screen.

Is this financial advice?

No. CoinhakoCrypto is a personal journaling tool. Nothing in the app constitutes financial advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any asset. You are responsible for your own decisions.
