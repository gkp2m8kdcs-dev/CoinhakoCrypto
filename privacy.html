Privacy Policy

CoinhakoCrypto is built around a simple idea: a private workspace for tracking your crypto portfolio. This policy explains exactly what data the app handles — and what it does not.

Last updated: September 20, 2026 · Effective immediately

Short version: CoinhakoCrypto does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. All your portfolio data lives locally on your device. The app does not require an account, does not connect to any server, and does not include analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs.

1. Overview

CoinhakoCrypto (the "App") is a personal crypto investment journal and portfolio tracker developed for iOS. The App is designed to operate offline after the initial setup. It does not maintain accounts, does not authenticate users, and does not transmit your portfolio data to any external server operated by the developer.

This Privacy Policy describes the data practices of CoinhakoCrypto. Because the App does not collect personal data from you, much of what is typically described in a privacy policy is intentionally short. We believe that is a feature.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data through CoinhakoCrypto. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any contact information.
  • Identifiers such as device advertising IDs (IDFA), advertising identifiers, or user IDs.
  • Location data, IP address, or network information.
  • Biometric data (the App does not use Face ID, Touch ID, or any biometric authentication).
  • Financial account credentials, exchange API keys, or wallet keys.
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, telemetry, or performance metrics tied to you.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

For clarity: the App does not collect information about your crypto holdings, transaction history, watchlists, or preferences. None of this data leaves CoinhakoCrypto unless you personally share an exported file (for example, a CSV) with someone else.

Manual exports only. The only way data leaves the App is when you, the user, actively export a CSV or text file via the iOS share sheet. Those exports are created and transmitted by you, not by CoinhakoCrypto.

4. Device Permissions

CoinhakoCrypto does not request any of the following iOS permissions:

  • Notifications (local or remote push).
  • Camera, Microphone, Photos, or Contacts.
  • Location Services (foreground or background).
  • Face ID / Touch ID / Biometric authentication.
  • Tracking permission under App Tracking Transparency (the App contains no tracking SDKs).
  • Background app refresh, cellular data, or any other optional system capability.

5. Local Storage

All data you create in CoinhakoCrypto — including transactions, holdings, watchlists, and preferences — is stored locally on your device using standard iOS data protection. The data is sandboxed within the App and is not accessible to other apps or to the developer.

Because data is stored locally only:

  • If you uninstall the App, your data is removed with it. Please export a backup beforehand if needed.
  • If you restore your device from a backup, the data may or may not be included depending on your iOS backup settings.
  • The developer cannot recover lost data, because the developer never receives it.

6. Third-Party Services

CoinhakoCrypto does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or marketing SDKs. It does not use Firebase, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, Adjust, AppsFlyer, Branch, or similar services. It does not embed third-party content that loads over the network.

The App does not connect to any cryptocurrency exchange, broker, custodian, or wallet provider. No order placement, authentication, or read-only API access is performed by the App.

7. Children's Privacy

CoinhakoCrypto is intended for users aged 16 and over (or older, depending on your jurisdiction). The App does not knowingly collect any information from children. Because no personal data is collected from anyone, this section exists primarily to confirm that we do not direct the App at children and do not maintain any age-gated data collection.

8. Your Rights & Choices

Because CoinhakoCrypto does not collect personal data from you, most data-subject rights (such as access, correction, or deletion requests) are not applicable — there is no data about you on any server we control.

However, you always have full control over the data on your device:

  • You can edit or delete any transaction, holding, or watchlist entry at any time.
  • You can reset app preferences from Settings → Reset Preferences.
  • You can export your data to CSV or text from Settings → Export.
  • You can uninstall the App to permanently remove all locally stored data.

9. Security

CoinhakoCrypto benefits from iOS's standard sandboxing and on-device data protection. All local data is isolated within the App's container and protected by the operating system. The App does not transmit data over the network, so there is no server-side attack surface related to your portfolio.

No security measure is perfect. You are responsible for maintaining the physical and passcode security of your device. We recommend enabling a strong device passcode and, where appropriate, biometric device unlock.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, surface a notice inside the App. Because the App does not collect contact information, we cannot notify you by email.

Material change principle. If a future version of CoinhakoCrypto were to introduce optional network-based features, this policy would be updated before such features shipped, and you would be asked to opt in.
